God forbid that we'll be sitting and teaching it and learning it and we become only spectators as you would use other people, but not us. Oh God, please grant us forgiveness of sin, not just unto salvation, but to be released, to be commissioned, to be empowered by you. To reach out to people everywhere with the gospel. In Jesus' name we pray, amen.
