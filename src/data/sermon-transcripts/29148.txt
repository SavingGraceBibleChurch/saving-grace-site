Help us, Lord, to understand the value, the preciousness of suffering for Jesus' sake.

That there is no greater glory than to suffer for Jesus in this life. That when we enter into heaven and all the eternal rewards we'll be getting, it is due to the suffering that we're willing to endure for Jesus' sake. The greatest ranking in your home, oh God, are exclusively given to those that are willing to suffer for you, Lord God, the most. Help us to adore this wonderful means to worship you, Lord. And in all that we say and do, Lord, may your name be glorified.
