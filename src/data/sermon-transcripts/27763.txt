Please God, we beg of you to teach us where our joy ought to lie. Not in the circumstances of life, not in the happiness that only comes when things go our way, but in Christ, who for the joy that was set before Him endured the cross. And Father, for those who do not know You, Lord, we pray that you give them no rest until rest and joy is found at Calvary. And we ask this in Jesus' name. Amen.
