But Lord God, we beg you today, as per the passage, that we would not be like the scribes, not seeing the mess, and therefore not dealing with the mess, but that we would see our mess the way you see it, so that we hand over the entirety of the mess to you, O God.

That you may change us, and that you may sanctify us and cleanse us, that we may live the entirety of our lives devoted to Jesus Christ and to Jesus alone. For to Him all glory. Amen.
