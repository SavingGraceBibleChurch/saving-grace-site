And because we are accepted in the Beloved, we don't need to pursue being accepted by a slanderer. Rather, give us the courage and the love for one another to protect one another and to withstand the slanderer and cut ourselves off of Him. That we would keep the gospel pure, and that we would advance your kingdom in this dying world. In Jesus' name, amen.
